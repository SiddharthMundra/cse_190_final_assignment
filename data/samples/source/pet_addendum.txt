This Pet Addendum ("Addendum") is attached to and forms part of the Residential Lease Agreement dated March 1, 2026, between Harborview Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Alexandra Nguyen ("Tenant") for Unit 4B, 892 Valencia Street, San Francisco, California 94110 (the "Premises"). Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings set forth in the Lease.

## 1. Approved Animals

Landlord approves Tenant to keep one (1) domestic cat named "Mochi," spayed/neutered, current on rabies vaccination, weighing no more than fifteen (15) pounds. No additional pets may be introduced without a written amendment. Tenant shall provide vaccination records within seven (7) days of move-in and annually thereafter.

## 2. Pet Deposits and Fees

In addition to the Security Deposit under the Lease, Tenant shall pay a refundable pet deposit of Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) per approved pet and a non-refundable pet administrative fee of Two Hundred Dollars ($200.00) due before the cat occupies the Premises. The pet deposit shall be held under the same trust requirements as the Security Deposit and may be applied to pet-related damage, professional carpet cleaning, flea treatment, or odor remediation exceeding ordinary wear.

## 3. Rules of Conduct

The approved cat must remain indoors except when carried in an enclosed carrier through common areas. Tenant shall not permit the cat on balconies without Landlord approval and a secured pet screen. Litter boxes must be cleaned daily and placed on waterproof mats. Tenant shall immediately pick up any waste in common areas. Excessive meowing or odor complaints may constitute a nuisance breach.

## 4. Liability and Insurance

Tenant assumes full liability for any injury, allergy complaint, or property damage caused by the approved cat. Tenant shall maintain renter's insurance covering pet liability of at least $300,000 and provide proof upon request. Landlord may require removal of the pet if two (2) verified bite or scratch incidents occur or if the cat is deemed a nuisance after written warning and a fourteen (14) day cure period.

## 5. Maintenance and Pest Control

Tenant shall pay for flea treatment of the Premises if an infestation is traced to the approved pet. Tenant authorizes quarterly pest inspections with forty-eight (48) hours' notice. Scratch damage to doors, screens, baseboards, or cabinetry shall be repaired at Tenant's expense before move-out.

## 6. Move-Out Procedures

Within five (5) days before vacating, Tenant shall professionally steam-clean all carpets and treat for fleas if recommended by Landlord's vendor. Failure to do so may result in deduction from the pet deposit and Security Deposit. Tenant shall patch nail holes from pet gates only with Landlord-approved materials.

## 7. Conflict with Lease

If any term of this Addendum conflicts with the Lease regarding pets, this Addendum controls solely with respect to approved animals. All other Lease terms remain in full force, including Rent, termination, and default provisions.

## 8. No Approval of Other Species

This Addendum does not authorize dogs, birds, reptiles, aquariums, or temporary pet-sitting of unapproved animals for more than forty-eight (48) hours. Temporary care requires prior email approval.

## 9. Emergency Veterinary Care

If the approved cat is found unattended and in distress, Landlord may enter the Premises after reasonable attempts to contact Tenant and may transport the cat to an emergency veterinarian at Tenant's expense, to be deducted from the pet deposit or billed separately.

## 10. Registration and Identification

Tenant shall register the approved cat with the City and County of San Francisco if required by local ordinance and shall provide Landlord a copy of the registration tag number within fourteen (14) days. The cat shall wear a collar with identification and rabies tag whenever in common areas inside a carrier.

## 11. Guests and Pet Sitters

Third-party pet sitters may care for the approved cat for up to fourteen (14) consecutive days if Tenant provides Landlord written notice including the sitter's contact information. Pet sitters must comply with all rules in this Addendum. Long-term pet sitting beyond fourteen (14) days requires Landlord approval.

## 12. Noise and Neighbor Complaints

Landlord will forward written neighbor complaints regarding the approved cat to Tenant. Tenant shall respond within forty-eight (48) hours with a remediation plan. Repeated substantiated complaints after two (2) warnings may be treated as a nuisance under Section 4.

## 13. Renewal and Move-Out of Pet

If Tenant renews the Lease, this Addendum renews automatically unless either Party gives thirty (30) days' notice to revoke pet approval. If the cat no longer resides at the Premises, Tenant shall notify Landlord in writing; pet deposit reconciliation follows Section 6 and applicable law.

## 14. Flooring and Window Treatments

Tenant shall install claw-resistant guards on any approved window screens and shall not install wall-to-wall carpet without Landlord consent. If the Premises include hardwood floors, Tenant shall place rugs under litter box areas and feeding stations to prevent moisture damage.

## 15. Building Access and Elevators

When transporting the approved cat through the lobby or elevators, Tenant shall use a hard-sided carrier with a latch. Tenant shall not allow the cat to walk unrestrained in common hallways, stairwells, or the garage. Violations may result in fines of $100 per incident after the first written warning.

## 16. Insurance Claims and Incidents

Tenant shall report any bite, scratch, or property damage involving the approved cat to Landlord within twenty-four (24) hours and shall cooperate with any insurance investigation. Failure to report incidents may result in immediate revocation of pet approval.

## 17. Addendum Termination

Landlord may terminate this Addendum independently of the Lease if Tenant materially breaches pet rules after notice and cure period. Termination of the Addendum requires removal of the cat within seven (7) days. Termination of the Lease automatically terminates this Addendum.
